# Glossary

**ACID** - Atomicity, Consistency, Isolation, and Durability, the four transaction-integrity properties. (Ch 3)
**Agile systems development** - Iterative delivery of small increments through collaboration, feedback, and adaptation. (Ch 13)
**Artificial intelligence (ANI/AGI/ASI)** - Narrow, general, and super intelligence stages; most current business AI is ANI. (Ch 11)
**Balanced Scorecard (BSC)** - Strategy measurement across financial, customer, internal-process, and innovation/learning perspectives. (Ch 12)
**Business impact analysis (BIA)** - Analysis of operational and financial consequences used to prioritize recovery and continuity. (Ch 5)
**Business intelligence (BI)** - Tools and practices that turn structured data into actionable reporting and insight. (Ch 6)
**Business-IT alignment** - Applying IT consistently and on time with business goals, strategy, and needs. (Ch 12)
**Business process management (BPM)** - Methods and tools for mapping, supporting, and continuously improving processes. (Ch 1)
**Business Process Reengineering (BPR)** - Radical redesign that removes obsolete work before automating the remaining process. (Ch 1)
**CAP theorem** - A distributed database cannot fully guarantee consistency, availability, and partition tolerance together. (Ch 3)
**CIDDA** - Confidence in Data-Dependent Assumptions: `G * M * TS`, confidence in data quality, meaning, and source. (Ch 3)
**Cloud computing/XaaS** - On-demand shared or private computing and service layers such as SaaS, PaaS, IaaS, DaaS, and TSaaS. (Ch 2)
**COBIT 2019** - IT governance and management framework that aligns objectives, controls, risk, and business needs. (Ch 5)
**Customer lifetime value (CLV)** - Value contributed across a customer relationship: average sale x repeat transactions x retention time. (Ch 10)
**Cyber defense strategies** - Prevention/deterrence, detection, containment, recovery, correction, and awareness/compliance. (Ch 5)
**Data governance** - Enterprise control of data availability, usability, integrity, and security. (Ch 3)
**Data, Information, Knowledge, and Wisdom** - Progression from raw facts to context, experience, and value-based judgment. (Ch 2)
**Data lake** - Repository retaining raw structured, semi-structured, and unstructured data before requirements are known. (Ch 3)
**Data warehouse/data mart** - Integrated analytical repository and its smaller function-specific form for OLAP. (Ch 3)
**Defense in depth** - Redundant controls across perimeter, network, host, application, and data layers. (Ch 5)
**Digital business model** - Revenue and value strategy using websites, social media, mobile devices, and related digital technology. (Ch 1)
**Digital transformation** - Business-model, process, and capability redesign using digital technology to improve performance and experience. (Ch 1)
**Edge computing** - Processing data near its source to reduce latency, traffic, and dependence on remote connectivity. (Ch 4)
**Electronic data interchange (EDI)** - Structured electronic exchange of business documents among authorized partners. (Ch 9, Ch 10)
**Enterprise architecture (EA)** - Blueprint connecting strategy, business, applications, information, technology, and transition sequencing. (Ch 2)
**Enterprise resource planning (ERP)** - Integrated process-management software using a shared database across functions. (Ch 10)
**ETL/CDC** - Extract-transform-load moves prepared data; change data capture propagates only source changes. (Ch 3)
**Functional business system (FBS)** - Information system designed to improve performance in one functional area. (Ch 9)
**Internet of Things (IoT)** - Networked physical objects with sensors, software, and connectivity that exchange data. (Ch 4)
**ITIL 4 Service Value System (SVS)** - Service-management model using four dimensions, guiding principles, and value-chain activities. (Ch 12)
**Lean Management** - Improvement cycle of Value, Map Value Stream, Create Flow, Establish Pull, and Seek Perfection. (Ch 9)
**Machine learning (ML)** - Algorithms that learn patterns or behavior from data through supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement training. (Ch 6, Ch 11)
**Master Data Management (MDM)** - Linking dispersed records into a consistent Master Reference File for shared business entities. (Ch 3)
**Omnichannel retailing** - Coordinated store, Web, and mobile channels that preserve customer, inventory, fulfillment, and service context. (Ch 8)
**Packet switching** - Sending messages as independently routed packets that are reassembled at the destination. (Ch 4)
**People-first technology** - Involving users throughout design and aligning technology value with user values, needs, and agency. (Ch 14)
**Prescriptive analytics** - Analysis that selects what should be done among actions under constraints. (Ch 6)
**Project Triple Constraint** - Scope, time, and cost constrain one another while quality reveals whether the trade-off works. (Ch 13)
**Quality of Service (QoS)** - Traffic prioritization and control based on latency, reliability, and business consequence. (Ch 4)
**Recommendation engine** - System that proactively predicts and presents content, products, people, or services a user may value. (Ch 7)
**SDLC** - Five-stage Systems Development Life Cycle: planning, analysis, design, implementation/testing, and support/maintenance. (Ch 13)
**Semantic Web** - Web of metadata, linked data, NLP, and AI that makes meaning and relationships computer-understandable. (Ch 7)
**SMAC** - Social-Mobile-Analytics-Cloud convergence for connected digital experiences. (Ch 1)
**Supply chain management (SCM)** - Management of material, information, and payment flows among supply-chain organizations. (Ch 10)
**Technology scanning** - Structured search and evaluation of emerging technologies against business and customer needs. (Ch 12)
**Triple Bottom Line** - Evaluation of profits, people, and planet together, including their interactions. (Ch 14)
**Virtualization** - Software separation of applications and resources from physical hardware so resources can be pooled. (Ch 2)
**Web 2.0 social strategy** - Listening, Talking, Energizing, Supporting, and Embracing as a two-way operating discipline. (Ch 7)
**Zero-day** - Exploit unknown to the organization until it is used in an attack. (Ch 5)
