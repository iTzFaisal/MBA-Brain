## Core Frameworks & Mental Models

- **Digital transformation and business models**: Start with customer expectations and the value proposition, not a technology purchase. Select among **Subscription**, **Freemium**, **Free**, **Access-over-Ownership**, **Experience**, **On-Demand**, and **Ecosystem** models according to how value is delivered and revenue is captured.
- **Business Process Reengineering (BPR)**: When a process is obsolete, slow, or handoff-heavy, use "Don't automate, obliterate!". Follow vision/objectives, understand the current process, select it for redesign, identify change levers, implement, operationalize, evaluate, and continuously improve.
- **Social-Mobile-Analytics-Cloud (SMAC)**: Design social influence, mobile context, analytics, and cloud access as one reinforcing experience rather than four disconnected features.
- **IS diagnosis and architecture**: Use the **IPOS cycle** (Input -> Processing -> Output -> Storage, with feedback) and the six components (hardware, software, people, procedures, network, data). Match **TPS**, **MIS**, **DSS**, or **EIS** to transaction, reporting, semistructured, or strategic decisions. Use **Enterprise Architecture** to connect baseline, target state, sequencing, and KPIs.
- **Cloud and infrastructure**: Choose **SaaS**, **PaaS**, **IaaS**, **DaaS**, or **TSaaS** by required control and management. Public cloud favors scale; private cloud favors control. Make security, recovery, performance, vendor dependence, and exit explicit in the **Cloud Service Agreement**.
- **Data trust and governance**: Separate operational truth (OLTP) from analytical truth (warehouse/OLAP). Use **ACID** for transactions, **ETL/CDC** for movement, **Master Data Management** for common entities, and governance for availability, usability, integrity, and security. Use `CIDDA = G * M * TS` to expose confidence in data quality, meaning, and source.
- **Networks, IoT, and edge**: Design IoT as collect -> prepare -> transmit -> analyze -> store -> report. Use edge processing for urgent, bandwidth-heavy, intermittently connected, or safety-critical action; use cloud for broad analysis and durable sharing. Apply **QoS** to prioritize traffic by consequence.
- **Cybersecurity and privacy**: Quantify `Expected loss = P1 * P2 * L`, then add a **Business Impact Analysis** for operational, regulatory, reputational, and recovery effects. Layer the **Cyber Defense Strategies**: prevention/deterrence, detection, containment, recovery, correction, and awareness/compliance. Use **Defense in Depth** across perimeter, network, host, application, and data.
- **Analytics and decision science**: Work through intelligence, design, choice, and review. Climb only as high as needed: **descriptive** (what happened), **predictive** (what could happen), then **prescriptive** (what should be done). Run the seven-stage lifecycle: capture, store, model, analyze, communicate, deploy, reiterate. Validate Volume, Variety, Velocity, and especially Veracity.
- **Social, semantic, and retail experience**: Use Web 2.0 **Listening, Talking, Energizing, Supporting, Embracing**. Move from keywords to intent with metadata, NLP, RDF/OWL/SPARQL, and access-controlled search. In **omnichannel retailing**, make store, Web, and mobile share customer, inventory, payment, fulfillment, and returns context.
- **Functional and enterprise systems**: Follow the process across functions, not just local metrics. Use Lean's **Value, Map Value Stream, Create Flow, Establish Pull, Seek Perfection**. Select ERP/SCM/CRM/EKM/ECM/ESP for integration needs, then govern fit, TCO, leadership, change, training, and adoption. In SCM, track material, information, and financial flows together.
- **AI, robotics, and quantum**: Most current business AI is **Artificial Narrow Intelligence**. Choose ML by training signal: supervised, unsupervised, or reinforcement. Use the **Gartner Five-Stage AI Maturity Model** from Awareness to Transformational; augment people before replacing them where explanation, empathy, safety, or accountability matters. Treat quantum's superposition, entanglement, and interference as a different, immature computing substrate.
- **IT strategy and sourcing**: Align business and IT through leadership, shared culture, and strategic/tactical/operational links. Use **Porter's Competitive Forces** and **Value Chain**, the five IT strategy components, **Balanced Scorecard**, **ITIL 4 Service Value System**, the five-phase outsourcing life cycle, and five-step technology scanning. Source capabilities, not merely labor, and make the SLA an operating control.
- **Systems and projects**: Use the five-stage **SDLC**, then choose Waterfall, Object-Oriented, Agile, or DevOps from requirements volatility and risk. Govern work through **Project, Program and Portfolio Management**, the **Project Triple Constraint**, five project phases, ten knowledge areas, WBS, baseline, risk register, critical path, and integrated change control. Report bad news early.
- **Ethics and sustainability**: Before deployment, name **Responsibility**, **Accountability**, and **Liability**. Apply the **Triple Bottom Line** (profits, people, planet), the four Rs (reduce, reuse, recycle, recover), the six quality-of-life elements, and a **people-first approach**. Treat privacy, accessibility, civil rights, labor transition, full lifecycle emissions, and user consent as requirements.
