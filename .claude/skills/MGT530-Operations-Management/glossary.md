# Glossary

Significant terms from the audited chapter files, alphabetized for lookup.

**ABC analysis** - Rank inventory by annual dollar value and focus control on the critical few (Ch 12).

**Aggregate planning** - Intermediate-range family-level plan balancing demand, capacity, workforce, inventory, and backlog (Ch 11).

**Availability** - Fraction of operating time an asset is usable; `MTBF/(MTBF + MTTR)` (Ch 4S, Ch 14S).

**Backward scheduling** - Start at the due date and calculate the latest feasible starts (Ch 16).

**Balanced Scorecard** - Strategy execution across financial, customer, internal-process, and learning/innovation measures (Ch 2).

**Bill of materials (BOM)** - Product-structure record showing each component and quantity per parent (Ch 13).

**Bottleneck** - Lowest-capacity operation that limits total system throughput (Ch 5, Ch 16, Ch 19).

**Capacity cushion** - Capacity deliberately held above expected demand to absorb uncertainty (Ch 5).

**Capacity utilization** - Actual output divided by design capacity; distinct from efficiency (Ch 5).

**Center of gravity** - Shipment-weighted coordinate for an initial distribution-location estimate (Ch 8).

**Concurrent engineering** - Cross-functional, overlapping design that exposes feasibility issues early (Ch 4).

**Control chart** - Time-ordered sample-statistic chart used to detect nonrandom process variation (Ch 3, Ch 10).

**Control limits** - Expected process bounds for sample statistics; not customer specifications (Ch 10).

**Cp/Cpk** - Capability indexes comparing stable process spread and centering with specifications (Ch 10).

**Critical path** - Longest project path; its activities have zero slack and determine duration (Ch 17).

**Cycle service level** - Probability that lead-time demand will not exceed available supply (Ch 12).

**Decision tree** - Sequential decision/chance diagram evaluated from right to left (Ch 5S).

**Delphi method** - Anonymous, iterative expert questionnaires used to develop consensus (Ch 3).

**Design capacity** - Maximum theoretical output under ideal conditions (Ch 5).

**Economic order quantity (EOQ)** - Order size minimizing basic ordering plus carrying cost: `sqrt(2DS/H)` (Ch 1, Ch 12).

**Efficiency** - Actual output divided by effective capacity (Ch 5).

**Exponential smoothing** - Forecast updated by prior forecast plus `alpha(actual - forecast)` (Ch 3).

**Factor rating** - Weighted comparison of location alternatives using quantitative and qualitative factors (Ch 8).

**Feasible region** - All decision-variable combinations satisfying every LP constraint and nonnegativity (Ch 19).

**Forecast error** - Actual minus forecast, `e = A - F`; sign shows under- or over-forecasting (Ch 3).

**Gross-to-net** - MRP procedure subtracting projected supply from gross requirements to find net need (Ch 13).

**Heijunka** - Lean leveling of workload and product mix to stabilize flow (Ch 14).

**Jidoka** - Autonomation: detect an abnormality and stop for quality-at-source correction (Ch 14).

**Kaizen** - Continuous, incremental improvement of methods, equipment, materials, and people (Ch 9, Ch 14).

**Kanban** - Pull signal authorizing replenishment or movement when downstream use occurs (Ch 14).

**Learning curve** - Model of declining human-work time as cumulative repetitions increase (Ch 7S).

**Linear programming (LP)** - Linear constrained optimization of a resource-allocation objective (Ch 19).

**Little's law** - Stable-system relationship `L = lambda W`; inventory/WIP equals rate times time (Ch 12, Ch 14, Ch 18).

**Load-distance** - Location screen `sum(load x distance)` for weighted movement (Ch 8).

**Master Production Schedule (MPS)** - Time-phased end-item plan that disaggregates aggregate production (Ch 11, Ch 13).

**MRP** - Dependent-demand planning that explodes the BOM, nets supply, and offsets releases by lead time (Ch 13).

**MTBF/MTTR** - Mean time between failure / mean time to repair; failure frequency and recovery inputs to availability (Ch 4S, Ch 14S).

**Order qualifier/order winner** - Minimum purchase requirement / characteristic that wins the order (Ch 2).

**Pareto effect** - A small number of causes often account for a disproportionate share of loss (Ch 1, Ch 9).

**PERT** - Probabilistic project estimates using `te = (to + 4tm + tp)/6` and variance `((tp-to)/6)^2` (Ch 17).

**Process capability** - Stable process ability to meet specifications; commonly assessed with Cp and Cpk (Ch 10).

**Quality at the source** - Each worker checks and corrects work before passing it to the next customer (Ch 9, Ch 14).

**Reorder point (ROP)** - Inventory trigger equal to expected lead-time demand plus safety stock (Ch 12).

**Reliability** - Probability of intended performance under stated conditions and time horizon (Ch 4, Ch 4S).

**Safety stock** - Inventory held to protect against demand or lead-time uncertainty (Ch 12, Ch 13).

**SCOR** - Supply-chain cycle: Plan, Source, Make, Deliver, Manage Returns (Ch 15).

**Six Sigma** - Data-based variation and defect reduction; DMAIC is Define, Measure, Analyze, Improve, Control (Ch 9).

**SMED** - Single-minute exchange of die; separate and convert internal setup work to reduce changeover time (Ch 6, Ch 14).

**Standard time** - Sustainable time for a qualified worker under specified methods, rating, and allowances (Ch 7).

**Takt time** - Available production time divided by demand; the pace needed to match customer need (Ch 14).

**Theory of Constraints (TOC)** - Identify, exploit, subordinate, elevate, and repeat at the system constraint (Ch 5, Ch 16).

**Total productive maintenance (TPM)** - Operator and specialist ownership of routine and complex equipment care (Ch 14S).

**Tracking signal** - Cumulative forecast error divided by MAD, used to detect bias (Ch 3).

**Value stream** - End-to-end sequence of value-creating and non-value-adding activities (Ch 1, Ch 14).

**Work sampling** - Random observations estimating activity proportions and delay rather than continuous timing (Ch 7).
