# Management Decision Cheatsheet

## Start With The Decision

| If you need to... | Use... | Ask first |
|---|---|---|
| Turn an aspiration into work | SMART + goal cascade | What result, owner, evidence, resources, and date? |
| Diagnose internal/external reality | SWOT | Which factors are internal capabilities versus external conditions? |
| Test durable resource advantage | VRIO | Is it valuable, rare, hard to imitate, and organized for use? |
| Choose how to compete | Five forces + generic strategy | Where are entrants, suppliers, buyers, substitutes, and rivalry strongest? |
| Align a new initiative | Strategic fit | What will we stop doing, and which activities reinforce this choice? |
| Evaluate an ethical dilemma | Four ethical lenses | What are the consequences, self-interest, rights, and justice implications? |
| Improve a weak decision | Rational model | Did we diagnose the gap, generate alternatives, evaluate, implement, and learn? |
| Decide whether data will help | Evidence-based management | What evidence is relevant, credible, missing, or misleading? |

## Quick Rules
- **When the goal is vague**, write a specific, measurable, attainable, results-oriented, time-bound outcome.
- **When a plan meets reality**, compare results with the goal and correct the plan or execution.
- **When a social issue intersects the business**, scan products, value chain, and local clusters for CSV.
- **When entering another country**, use a geocentric mindset; research language, context, law, politics, time, space, and religion.
- **When culture looks healthy**, compare artifacts and stated values with rewards, crisis responses, systems, and actual behavior.
- **When changing culture**, use multiple levers: leaders, stories, rituals, structure, goals, rewards, hiring, training, and systems.
- **When HR is a bottleneck**, align recruitment, selection, onboarding, development, compensation, performance, and employee relations.
- **When motivation is low**, check needs, fairness, expectancy, rewards, job design, resources, and stress before blaming attitude.
- **When a group is not performing**, inspect purpose, composition, roles, norms, trust, goals, feedback, accountability, and process.
- **When conflict is too low**, introduce dissent or structured debate; when it is too high, use cooling, compromise, or integration.
- **When the situation is ambiguous or emotional**, use a rich medium and verify understanding with questions and paraphrase.
- **When information is missing**, expect the grapevine to fill the gap; communicate quickly and transparently.
- **When quality is unstable**, use PDCA: Plan, Do, Check, Act. Improve the system before blaming a worker.
- **When control is needed**, use feedforward before work, concurrent during work, and feedback after work.
- **When measuring performance**, include financial, customer, internal-process, and innovation/learning perspectives.
- **When a project needs structure**, flowchart sequence, Gantt-chart timing, and break-even-test economics.

## Choose The Management Lens

| Symptom | First lens | Common smell |
|---|---|---|
| Slow or wasteful work | Classical/operations | No standard method or unclear responsibility |
| Low morale or effort | Behavioral/motivation | Needs, fairness, or meaning ignored |
| Conflicting departments | Systems | Local optimization and weak feedback |
| Different people/situations | Contingency | One policy applied regardless of context |
| Stale routines | Learning/change | Evidence is collected but behavior never changes |
| Strategy not executing | Culture-structure-HR | Stated strategy conflicts with incentives or capabilities |

## Decision Hygiene
- Name the actual problem before generating solutions.
- Invite minority dissent; use a devil's advocate or dialectic method for consequential choices.
- Watch for availability, representativeness, confirmation, sunk cost, anchoring, overconfidence, hindsight, framing, escalation, and categorical-thinking biases.
- Treat intuition as expertise-dependent evidence, not a universal shortcut.
- Separate legal permission from ethical responsibility.
- Make assumptions, measures, owners, and correction triggers visible.
