# Glossary

**Abnormal spoilage** - Waste beyond the expected level, treated as a loss (Ch 3).
**Absorption costing** - Product costing that includes fixed manufacturing overhead (Ch 7).
**Activity base** - Measurable usage used to assign an activity cost pool (Ch 4).
**Activity-based costing (ABC)** - Assigns overhead through activities, pools, and drivers (Ch 4-5).
**Activity rate** - Budgeted activity cost divided by estimated driver usage (Ch 4).
**Applied overhead** - Job or product activity multiplied by a predetermined overhead rate (Ch 2).
**Budgetary slack** - Cushion created by understating sales or overstating resource needs (Ch 8).
**Balanced scorecard (BSC)** - Performance system balancing financial and nonfinancial perspectives (Ch 14).
**Bottleneck** - Constrained resource limiting output; rank products by margin per bottleneck hour (Ch 11).
**Break-even point** - Activity level at which operating income is zero (Ch 6).
**Capital budgeting** - Planning and evaluating long-term fixed-asset investments (Ch 12).
**Cash equivalents** - Short-term, highly liquid investments readily convertible to cash (Ch 15).
**Cash payback** - Time required for cumulative cash inflows to recover an investment (Ch 12).
**COGS** - Cost of finished goods sold during the period (Ch 1-3).
**COGM** - Beginning WIP plus manufacturing costs less ending WIP (Ch 1).
**Common-size analysis** - Expresses statement items as percentages of a common base (Ch 16).
**Contribution margin** - Sales less all variable costs (Ch 6-7).
**Conversion costs** - Direct labor plus factory overhead (Ch 1-3).
**Controllable cost** - Cost a manager can influence at the relevant level (Ch 9-10).
**Cost driver** - Activity measure that explains resource consumption (Ch 4-5).
**Cost object** - Product, job, department, customer, or other item receiving a cost (Ch 1).
**Cost of production report** - Process-costing report allocating cost to transfers and ending WIP (Ch 3).
**Cost center** - Responsibility center accountable primarily for costs (Ch 10).
**Differential analysis** - Comparison of future revenues and costs that differ between alternatives (Ch 11).
**Direct cost** - Economically traceable to a cost object (Ch 1).
**Direct method** - Cash-flow method listing operating receipts and payments (Ch 15).
**Direct materials** - Materials traceable to a product or job (Ch 1-3).
**Direct labor** - Labor traceable to a product or job (Ch 1-3).
**Economic value added/residual income** - Operating income above the required return on assets (Ch 10).
**Equivalent unit** - One fully completed unit of work for a cost category (Ch 3).
**Factory overhead** - Manufacturing costs other than direct materials and direct labor (Ch 1-2).
**FIFO process costing** - Cost-flow method isolating current-period work and cost (Ch 3).
**Financial accounting** - General-purpose external reporting, normally under GAAP (Ch 1).
**Flexible budget** - Budget adjusted to the actual activity level (Ch 8-9).
**Free cash flow** - Operating cash flow less cash used to purchase PP&E (Ch 15).
**Gross profit** - Sales less cost of goods sold (Ch 1).
**Hurdle rate** - Minimum desired return for an investment (Ch 12).
**Indirect cost** - Cost not economically traceable to one cost object (Ch 1).
**Indirect method** - Cash-flow method reconciling net income to operating cash flow (Ch 15).
**Internal failure cost** - Quality cost from defects found before delivery (Ch 13).
**Joint cost** - Shared cost incurred before multiple outputs reach split-off (Ch 5).
**Job cost sheet** - Subsidiary record of materials, labor, and overhead for a job (Ch 2).
**Job order costing** - Cost accumulation by separately identifiable job or batch (Ch 2).
**Lagging indicator** - Measure that is predicted by another performance measure (Ch 14).
**Lead time** - Time from production start to completion (Ch 13).
**Liquidity** - Ability to pay short-term obligations (Ch 16).
**Margin of safety** - Sales above break-even that can be lost before a loss (Ch 6).
**Master budget** - Integrated operating and financial budget (Ch 8).
**Mixed cost** - Cost containing fixed and variable components (Ch 6).
**Net present value (NPV)** - Present value of inflows less initial investment (Ch 12).
**Net realizable value (NRV)** - Final sales value less separable processing costs (Ch 5).
**Non-value-added activity** - Activity caused by waste, error, or process failure (Ch 13).
**Operating leverage** - Contribution margin divided by operating income (Ch 6).
**Opportunity cost** - Contribution forgone by choosing one use of a scarce resource (Ch 11).
**Overapplied overhead** - Applied overhead exceeds actual overhead (Ch 2).
**Payback period** - See cash payback (Ch 12).
**Performance metric** - Imperfect representation of a strategic objective (Ch 14).
**Period cost** - Selling or administrative cost expensed when incurred (Ch 1).
**Predetermined overhead rate** - Estimated overhead divided by estimated activity (Ch 2).
**Prime cost** - Direct materials plus direct labor (Ch 1).
**Process costing** - Cost accumulation by department for homogeneous continuous output (Ch 3).
**Product cost** - Manufacturing cost inventoried until the product is sold (Ch 1).
**Profit center** - Responsibility center accountable for revenues and costs (Ch 10).
**Quick ratio** - Quick assets divided by current liabilities (Ch 16).
**Relevant cost** - Future cost that differs among alternatives (Ch 11).
**Residual income** - Operating income minus required return on invested assets (Ch 10).
**Responsibility accounting** - Reports performance in units matched to authority (Ch 8, 10).
**Return on investment (ROI)** - Operating income divided by invested assets (Ch 10).
**Sales mix** - Relative proportions of products sold (Ch 6-7).
**Sunk cost** - Past cost that cannot be recovered or changed by a decision (Ch 11).
**Split-off point** - Point where joint products become separately identifiable (Ch 5).
**Standard cost** - Attainable target for price, rate, quantity, or time (Ch 9).
**Strategy map** - Expected cause-and-effect links among scorecard objectives (Ch 14).
**Surrogation** - Treating a performance measure as if it were the strategic objective (Ch 14).
**Target costing** - Expected market price less desired profit equals allowable cost (Ch 11).
**Transfer price** - Internal price charged between divisions (Ch 10).
**Underapplied overhead** - Actual overhead exceeds applied overhead (Ch 2).
**Value-added activity** - Activity necessary to meet customer requirements (Ch 13).
**Variable costing** - Product costing that includes only variable manufacturing costs (Ch 7).
**Variable cost** - Cost that changes in total with activity but is constant per unit (Ch 6).
**Vertical analysis** - Expresses statement items as percentages of a significant total (Ch 16).
**Working capital** - Current assets less current liabilities (Ch 16).
