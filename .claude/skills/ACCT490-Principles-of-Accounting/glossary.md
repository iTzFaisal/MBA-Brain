# Glossary

**Accounting equation** - Assets = Liabilities + Equity; the expanded form includes common stock, dividends, revenues, and expenses (Ch 1).
**Accounts payable** - Amounts owed to suppliers for credit purchases (Ch 2, 9).
**Accounts receivable** - Customer claims created by credit sales (Ch 7).
**Accrual basis** - Recognizes revenue when earned and expense when incurred, regardless of cash timing (Ch 3).
**Allowance method** - Estimates uncollectible receivables in the sales period and uses a contra-asset allowance (Ch 7).
**Amortization** - Allocation of a limited-life intangible asset or debt discount/premium over time (Ch 8, 10).
**Assets** - Controlled resources expected to provide future benefits (Ch 1).
**Available-for-sale (AFS)** - Securities measured at fair value with unrealized changes reported in other comprehensive income under the source framework (App C).
**Bank reconciliation** - Report that explains differences between bank and book Cash and identifies book-side entries (Ch 6).
**Bond** - Written promise to pay principal at maturity and contract-rate interest (Ch 10).
**Book value** - Cost less accumulated depreciation, depletion, or amortization (Ch 8, 10).
**Cash equivalents** - Highly liquid investments convertible to a known cash amount and normally acquired within three months of maturity (Ch 6, 12).
**Common-size analysis** - Expresses balance-sheet items as a percent of assets and income-statement items as a percent of sales (Ch 13).
**Contingent liability** - Potential obligation arising from a past event and dependent on an uncertain future event (Ch 9).
**Cost of goods sold (COGS)** - Inventory cost matched with merchandise sales (Ch 4, 5).
**Current ratio** - Current assets divided by current liabilities (Ch 3, 13).
**Debt ratio** - Total liabilities divided by total assets (Ch 2, 13).
**Depreciation** - Allocation of a depreciable plant-asset cost over its useful life; not a market-value measure (Ch 3, 8).
**Double-entry accounting** - Every transaction records equal total debits and credits affecting at least two accounts (Ch 2).
**Equity method** - Records the investor's share of investee income and reduces the investment for dividends (App C).
**FIFO** - Cost-flow assumption assigning earliest costs to COGS and recent costs to ending inventory (Ch 5).
**Fraud Triangle** - Fraud risk arises from opportunity, pressure, and rationalization (Ch 1, 6).
**Gross margin** - Net sales less COGS; also called gross profit (Ch 4, 13).
**Impairment** - Required write-down when an asset's carrying amount exceeds the applicable recoverable or fair amount (Ch 8).
**Internal control** - Policies and procedures protecting assets, improving reliability, and supporting authorized operations (Ch 6).
**Inventory turnover** - COGS divided by average inventory (Ch 5, 13).
**LIFO** - Cost-flow assumption assigning recent costs to COGS and older costs to ending inventory (Ch 5).
**Market rate** - Investor-required return used to price debt and calculate effective-interest expense (Ch 10).
**Net realizable value** - Expected cash collection or selling price less costs needed to complete the sale (Ch 5, 7).
**Operating cycle** - Time from acquiring assets for operations to collecting cash from their sale (Ch 1, 3, 9).
**Present value (PV)** - Current equivalent of a future amount discounted at a required rate (App B, Ch 10).
**Profit margin** - Net income divided by net sales (Ch 3, 13).
**Retained earnings** - Cumulative income less losses and dividends retained in the business (Ch 1, 11).
**Return on assets (ROA)** - Net income divided by average total assets; can be decomposed into margin times turnover (Ch 1, 13, App C).
**Return on equity (ROE)** - Net income available to common owners divided by average common equity (Ch 13).
**Significant influence** - Usually 20%-50% voting ownership, normally accounted for by the equity method (App C).
**Solvency** - Long-run ability to generate revenue and meet obligations (Ch 13).
**Times interest earned** - Income before interest and taxes divided by interest expense (Ch 9, 13).
**Treasury stock** - Corporation's reacquired shares reported as a deduction from equity (Ch 11).
**Unearned revenue** - Liability for cash received before the company performs (Ch 1-4, 9).
**Weighted average** - Inventory method using one average unit cost for goods available (Ch 5).
