# Finance Decision Aid

## Five Principles

| If the question is about... | Apply |
|---|---|
| What changes because of the decision? | **Cash Flow Is What Matters**; use incremental cash flow. |
| Cash at different dates | **Money Has a Time Value**; timeline, then discount or compound. |
| Uncertainty | **Risk Requires a Reward**; match return to relevant risk. |
| A quoted price or rate | **Market Prices Are Generally Right**; use current opportunity cost as the benchmark. |
| Ownership versus control | **Conflicts of Interest Cause Agency Problems**; align incentives and monitor. |

## Project Acceptance and Ranking

| Situation | Rule |
|---|---|
| Independent project | Accept if `NPV >= 0`, equivalently valid `IRR >= k` or `PI >= 1`. |
| Mutually exclusive projects | Choose the largest NPV. |
| Capital rationing | Choose the feasible combination with the largest total NPV. |
| Unequal lives | Use replacement chain or EAA before ranking. |
| Multiple cash-flow sign changes | Do not rely on ordinary IRR; use NPV or MIRR. |
| Quick liquidity screen | Payback or discounted payback only; never replace NPV. |

## Valuation and Risk

- Draw a timeline; use matching periods and signs.
- `PV = FV/(1+r)^n`; `FV = PV(1+r)^n`; compare different APRs with EAR.
- Bond value = PV(coupons) + PV(par). Required return above coupon rate means a discount bond.
- Preferred stock: `V = D/r`. Constant-growth common stock: `V = D1/(r-g)` only when `r > g`.
- CAPM hurdle: `rf + beta(rm-rf)`. Diversification removes unsystematic, not systematic, risk.
- WACC: `wd*after-tax kd + wps*kps + wcs*kcs`; use a divisional WACC when project risk differs.

## Financing and Dividend

- If business or operating risk is high, be cautious about adding financial leverage.
- Tax shield benefit is not free: weigh distress, agency, covenant, refinancing, and flexibility costs.
- `break-even units = fixed costs/(price - variable cost)`; `DTL = DOL * DFL`.
- Fund positive-NPV projects and the target mix first; retain earnings before issuing costly new equity.
- Then use residual dividend logic, subject to liquidity, covenants, law, taxes, control, and expectations.
- Treat EPS as a sensitivity measure, not the objective; shareholder wealth and risk decide.

## Working Capital and Credit

| If... | Then... |
|---|---|
| Forecasting growth | `DFN = change in assets - change in spontaneous liabilities - change in retained earnings`. |
| Timing is seasonal | Build a cash budget by receipt and payment month, including minimum cash and borrowing. |
| Cash is trapped in operations | `CCC = DSO + DSI - DPO`; collect faster, turn inventory faster, or extend supplier terms responsibly. |
| Comparing short-term loans | Use usable proceeds and all fees, discounts, and compensating balances; compare APR/APY and availability. |
| Funding a temporary asset | Use temporary, self-liquidating debt; fund permanent assets with permanent financing. |
| Choosing inventory order size | Use `EOQ = sqrt(2SO/C)` only under its assumptions; add safety stock for uncertainty. |

## International Finance

- Label currency units. A direct quote is home currency per foreign unit; invert only when the desired units require it.
- Use spot for immediate settlement and forward for a known future rate; forward is not a guaranteed future spot forecast.
- If quoted markets violate a cross-rate or IRP benchmark after transaction costs, buy cheap and sell dear.
- Use PPP and the law of one price for long-run goods-price checks, and the International Fisher effect to avoid chasing nominal yield alone.
- For DFI, value parent-currency cash actually repatriated after local taxes, blocked funds, political limits, and FX conversion.
