# Finance Glossary

**After-tax cost of debt** - Before-tax debt cost multiplied by `(1 - tax rate)` when interest is deductible (Ch 9).
**Agency problem** - Conflict when managers' interests differ from owners' interests (Ch 1).
**Annuity** - Equal cash flows for fixed periods; an annuity due pays at period beginning (Ch 5).
**Arbitrage** - Buying in a cheaper market and selling in a dearer one to capture a riskless price gap (Ch 16).
**Asset depreciation range (ADR)** - Classification approach used to assign a tax recovery period (Appendix 11A).
**Beta** - Slope of a security's returns against market returns; a measure of systematic risk (Ch 6).
**Bond** - Debt claim promising periodic interest and repayment of par value at maturity (Ch 7).
**Break-even quantity** - Units at which operating income is zero: `fixed costs / (price - variable cost)` (Ch 12).
**Capital budgeting** - Choosing whether to accept, reject, replace, or expand long-term asset investments (Ch 10).
**Capital structure** - Long-term mix of interest-bearing debt, preferred stock, and common equity (Ch 9, Ch 12).
**Cash budget** - Schedule of cash receipts, disbursements, minimum balance, and financing (Ch 14).
**Cash conversion cycle (CCC)** - Days cash is tied up: `DSO + DSI - DPO` (Ch 15, Ch 17).
**Common-size statement** - Statement with each item expressed as a percentage of sales or total assets (Ch 3, Ch 4).
**Constant-growth dividend model** - Common-stock value `D1/(r-g)` when growth is constant and `r > g` (Ch 8).
**Cost of capital** - Return demanded by the firm's capital suppliers as an opportunity cost (Ch 9).
**Depreciation tax shield** - Tax reduction from a deductible noncash depreciation expense (Ch 11, Appendix 11A).
**Direct foreign investment (DFI)** - Physical investment by a firm in another country (Ch 16).
**Discretionary financing need (DFN)** - Funding requiring active financing: change in assets less spontaneous liabilities and retained earnings (Ch 14).
**Dividend payout ratio** - Dividends per share divided by earnings per share (Ch 13).
**Divisional WACC** - Risk-adjusted cost of capital for a business unit (Ch 9).
**EBIT** - Operating income before interest and taxes (Ch 3).
**EBIT-EPS indifference point** - EBIT at which two financing plans produce the same EPS (Ch 12).
**Economic order quantity (EOQ)** - Order size balancing carrying and ordering costs: `sqrt(2SO/C)` (Ch 17).
**Effective annual rate (EAR)** - Annual compound rate used to compare different compounding frequencies (Ch 5).
**Financial leverage** - Fixed financial costs amplify changes in EBIT into changes in EPS (Ch 12).
**Flotation costs** - Issuance fees reducing usable security proceeds (Ch 2, Ch 9).
**Free cash flow (FCF)** - Cash left after operations, NOWC, and long-term investment (Appendix 3A, Ch 11).
**Holding-period return** - Realized dollar gain divided by beginning investment (Ch 6).
**Incremental cash flow** - Difference between cash flows with and without a decision (Ch 1, Ch 11).
**Interest rate parity (IRP)** - No-arbitrage relation linking forward/spot FX rates to national interest rates (Ch 16).
**Internal growth** - Growth from retaining and reinvesting profits; `ROE * retention rate` (Ch 8).
**Internal rate of return (IRR)** - Discount rate that makes NPV equal to zero (Ch 10, Appendix A).
**MACRS** - Tax-depreciation system that accelerates deductions by recovery class and year (Appendix 11A).
**Marketable securities** - Liquid, low-risk investments used for temporary excess cash (Ch 17).
**Net operating working capital (NOWC)** - Operating current assets less non-interest-bearing operating liabilities (Appendix 3A).
**Net present value (NPV)** - Present value of future FCF less initial outlay; accept an independent project when `NPV >= 0` (Ch 10).
**Operating leverage** - Fixed operating costs amplify sales changes into EBIT changes (Ch 12).
**Operating return on assets (OROA)** - EBIT divided by total assets; margin multiplied by asset turnover (Ch 4).
**Opportunity cost** - Value of the best alternative forgone by a decision (Ch 1, Ch 11).
**Payback period** - Time required for undiscounted FCF to recover the initial outlay (Ch 10).
**Perpetuity** - Equal cash flow continuing indefinitely; value of a constant payment is `payment / rate` (Ch 5, Ch 8).
**Preferred stock** - Fixed-dividend equity claim senior to common stock but junior to debt (Ch 8).
**Profitability index (PI)** - PV of future project FCF divided by initial outlay (Ch 10).
**Purchasing-power parity (PPP)** - Long-run exchange-rate benchmark based on comparable goods prices (Ch 16).
**Real option** - Value of management's ability to delay, expand, contract, or abandon a project (Ch 11).
**Repatriation** - Bringing foreign subsidiary cash back to the parent company (Ch 16).
**Residual dividend theory** - Pay dividends only after funding positive-NPV investments and the equity requirement (Ch 13).
**Risk premium** - Return above the risk-free rate required for bearing risk (Ch 2, Ch 6).
**Shareholder wealth maximization** - Maximize market value of existing common stock (Ch 1).
**Spontaneous financing** - Operating payables and accruals that arise automatically with activity (Ch 14, Ch 15).
**Systematic risk** - Market-wide, nondiversifiable risk measured in CAPM by beta (Ch 6).
**Tax shield** - Tax saving created by a deductible expense, especially interest or depreciation (Ch 11, Ch 12, Appendix 11A).
**Time value of money (TVM)** - A dollar today is worth more than a later dollar because it can earn a return (Ch 5).
**Times interest earned** - EBIT divided by interest expense; a coverage measure (Ch 4).
**Total leverage** - Combined amplification of sales changes into EPS changes: `DTL = DOL * DFL` (Ch 12).
**Unsystematic risk** - Company-specific risk that diversification can largely remove (Ch 6).
**Weighted average cost of capital (WACC)** - Weighted average of after-tax debt, preferred, and common-equity costs (Ch 9).
**Yield to maturity (YTM)** - Rate equating a bond's price with its promised coupons and maturity value (Ch 7).
