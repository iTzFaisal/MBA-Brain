# Glossary

**Absolute system** - Rates performance against a predefined standard rather than against peers (Ch 5).

**Accountability** - Broad area of job responsibility for producing results (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Adverse impact** - An apparently neutral practice that disproportionately disadvantages a protected group (Ch 10).

**Behavioral indicator** - Observable action used as evidence of an underlying competency (Ch 5).

**Behaviorally Anchored Rating Scale (BARS)** - Rating scale whose levels are anchored with concrete critical incidents (Ch 5).

**Broad banding** - Combining many job grades into a small number of wide pay bands (Ch 10).

**Coaching** - Collaborative, ongoing direction, support, feedback, correction, and development (Ch 9).

**Competency** - Measurable cluster of knowledge, skills, and abilities that influences performance (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Contingent pay** - Pay increase or variable reward tied wholly or partly to performance (Ch 1, Ch 10).

**Context congruence** - Fit between a PM system and the organization's cultural and environmental context (Ch 1).

**Contextual performance** - Extra-role behavior that supports the social and psychological work environment (Ch 4, Ch 11).

**Critical incident** - Concrete example of especially effective or ineffective job behavior (Ch 5, Ch 7).

**Developmental activity** - Training, mentoring, reading, rotation, or assignment used to achieve a development objective (Ch 8).

**Developmental plan / IDP** - Joint plan for improving performance, sustaining strengths, and preparing for future roles (Ch 8).

**Disparate treatment** - Intentional differential treatment based on a protected or nonperformance characteristic (Ch 10).

**Employee agency** - The employee's role as source and director of behavioral change in coaching (Ch 9).

**Expectancy-Instrumentality-Valence model** - Motivation depends on belief that effort produces performance, performance produces reward, and the reward is valued (Ch 10).

**Feedforward Interview (FFI)** - Interview that identifies conditions behind prior success and converts them into future actions (Ch 8).

**Feedback gap** - Mutual avoidance of negative feedback that falsely signals acceptable performance (Ch 9).

**Frame of reference** - Shared understanding of what performance levels look like on a dimension (Ch 7).

**Forced distribution** - Predetermined proportions assigned to performance categories (Ch 5).

**Gain sharing** - Group or organizational reward linked to profitability or savings (Ch 10).

**Goal cascading** - Translating organizational priorities into unit, job, team, and individual expectations (Ch 2, Ch 3).

**Job analysis** - Systematic identification of job activities, outputs, processes, and requirements (Ch 2, Ch 3).

**Job evaluation** - Ranking, classifying, or point-scoring jobs to establish relative worth (Ch 10).

**KSAs** - Knowledge, skills, and abilities required for effective job performance (Ch 2, Ch 3, Ch 4).

**Mechanical scoring** - Combining weighted ratings according to a published formula (Ch 6).

**Me/We model** - Joint measurement of individual task performance, individual team contribution, and whole-team performance (Ch 11).

**Mission** - Present-oriented statement of why an organization exists and what it does (Ch 3).

**Performance appraisal** - Systematic description of employee strengths and weaknesses; one component of PM (Ch 1).

**Performance management (PM)** - Continuous identification, measurement, development, and strategic alignment of performance (Ch 1).

**Performance standard** - Yardstick specifying how well an objective must be achieved (Ch 2, Ch 5).

**Procedural justice** - Perceived fairness of rating and reward procedures (Ch 1).

**Psychological safety** - Climate in which people can provide feedback and admit weaknesses without retaliation (Ch 8).

**Rating distortion** - Intentional or unintentional inflation or deflation of ratings (Ch 6, Ch 7).

**Results approach** - Measures outcomes produced rather than how work is performed (Ch 4, Ch 5).

**Reward system** - Mechanism for distributing tangible and intangible employment returns (Ch 1).

**Strategic congruence** - Alignment among organizational, unit, team, job, and individual priorities (Ch 1, Ch 3).

**Strategic consensus** - Agreement on common strategies across organizational units (Ch 3).

**Task performance** - Role-prescribed activities that produce or enable goods and services (Ch 4, Ch 11).

**Trait approach** - Measures relatively stable attributes rather than behavior, situation, or results (Ch 4).

**Two-level appeals process** - HR mediation followed by independent panel review when necessary (Ch 7).

**Validity** - Degree to which a measure represents relevant performance and supports the intended decision (Ch 1, Ch 4).

**Vision** - Future-oriented description of what an organization aspires to become (Ch 3).

**Voice behavior** - Constructive challenge or suggestion intended to improve the organization (Ch 4).

**720-degree feedback** - Repeated 360-degree feedback used to monitor change over time (Ch 8).
