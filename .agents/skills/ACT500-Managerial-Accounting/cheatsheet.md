# Decision Cheatsheet

## Cost Flow and Classification
| If you need to... | Use |
|---|---|
| Cost a custom job | Job order costing; DM + DL + applied FOH |
| Cost homogeneous continuous output | Process costing; equivalent units and cost per EU |
| Classify product costs | Product = DM + DL + FOH; period = selling + administrative |
| Compute COGM | Beginning WIP + manufacturing costs - ending WIP |
| Compute COGS | Beginning FG + COGM - ending FG |

## Allocation Choice
- One similar product/resource pattern: plantwide rate.
- Different production departments: departmental rates.
- Different setups, inspections, engineering, or customer support: ABC.
- Support services immaterial: direct method.
- Some inter-support services matter: sequential method.
- All reciprocal services matter and benefit exceeds complexity: reciprocal method.
- Joint cost decision: allocate for reporting, but use only separable/incremental costs for process-further decisions.

## CVP Rules
| Question | Formula |
|---|---|
| Unit contribution margin | Selling price - variable cost/unit |
| Contribution-margin ratio | Contribution margin / sales |
| Break-even units | Fixed costs / unit contribution margin |
| Break-even dollars | Fixed costs / contribution-margin ratio |
| Target-profit units | (Fixed costs + target profit) / unit contribution margin |
| Operating leverage | Contribution margin / operating income |
| Margin of safety | Actual sales - break-even sales |

Use unit margin for unit changes and the ratio for dollar-sales changes. Rebuild the composite product when sales mix changes.

## Budget and Variance Rules
- Sales budget drives production; production drives materials, labor, and overhead.
- Activity changed? Use a flexible budget, not a static comparison.
- Materials: price variance `(AP - SP) x AQ`; quantity variance `(AQ - SQ) x SP`.
- Labor: rate variance `(AR - SR) x AH`; time variance `(AH - SH) x SR`.
- Investigate significant or suspiciously favorable variances; assign only controllable causes.

## Decision Analysis
- Future and different: relevant.
- Past and unrecoverable: sunk, exclude.
- Fixed and unchanged: exclude.
- Best alternative forgone: opportunity cost, include.
- Special order with idle capacity: accept when incremental revenue exceeds incremental cost.
- Bottleneck: rank contribution margin per constrained hour.
- Normal price: market price, full cost recovery, and desired profit.
- Target cost: expected market price - desired profit.

## Decentralization and Investment
- Cost center -> budget performance report.
- Profit center -> controllable revenue and cost/income.
- Investment center -> ROI, margin, turnover, and residual income.
- ROI = operating income / invested assets.
- Residual income = operating income - required return on assets.
- Transfer with excess capacity: variable cost < negotiated price < market price.

## Capital Budgeting
- ARR = average accounting income / average investment.
- Payback = time to recover initial cash outflow.
- NPV = PV of cash inflows - investment; accept if `NPV >= 0`.
- PVI = PV of cash flows / investment; useful under capital rationing.
- IRR >= hurdle rate: accept.
- Equalize project lives before comparing NPVs; sensitivity-test uncertain cash flows.

## Lean and Scorecards
- Reduce inventory to expose problems, then remove causes.
- Reduce setup before shrinking batch size.
- Pull means downstream demand controls upstream production.
- Quality cost = prevention + appraisal + internal failure + external failure.
- BSC chain: strategy -> objectives -> metrics -> initiatives -> targets -> tested relationships.
- Treat strategy maps as hypotheses; watch for surrogation and common-measures bias.

## Cash and Ratios
- Cash change = CFO + CFI + CFF.
- Indirect CFO: net income + noncash expenses - gains + losses - increases in operating assets + increases in operating liabilities.
- Free cash flow = CFO - PP&E purchases.
- Current ratio = current assets / current liabilities.
- Quick ratio = quick assets / current liabilities.
- ROA, ROE, turnover, and days measures require context and usually average balances.
