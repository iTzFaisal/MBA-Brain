# Leadership Development Cheatsheet

## Start With The Diagnosis

| If the question is... | Start with... | Default move |
|---|---|---|
| Is this leadership? | Process test | Find influence, group, reciprocal interaction, and common goal. |
| Who fits this role? | Trait-role fit | Define situational demands; compare evidence, not charisma alone. |
| What can be developed? | Three skills | Build technical, human, or conceptual capability through practice. |
| What behavior is missing? | Behavioral audit | Add task structure, relationship support, or both. |
| What does this follower need now? | SLII | Match D1/D2/D3/D4 to S1/S2/S3/S4. |
| Why is motivation blocked? | Path-goal | Repair expectancy, instrumentality, valence, or an obstacle. |
| Why are dyads unequal? | LMX/leadership making | Offer everyone a fair chance at expanded roles and reciprocity. |
| Does the work require changed values or habits? | Adaptive leadership | Shift the work to followers; do not supply a technical fix. |
| Is meaning, morality, and commitment the issue? | Transformational/authentic/servant | Test shared purpose, dissent, development, service, and collective benefit. |
| Is the team stuck? | Hill Model | Monitor -> classify task/relational and internal/external -> act -> re-monitor. |
| Is advancement inequitable? | Labyrinth/role congruity | Locate the barrier and make criteria, access, and reviewers accountable. |
| Is culture shaping the response? | GLOBE | Ask before stereotyping; adapt expression, not ethical foundations. |

## Fast Decision Rules

- If the problem has a known solution inside existing authority, use a technical response. If people must learn but the goal is clear, it is mixed. If values, identity, roles, or behavior must shift, treat it as adaptive.
- For SLII: D1 = low competence/high commitment -> S1 directing; D2 = some competence/low commitment -> S2 coaching; D3 = capable/uncertain -> S3 supporting; D4 = high competence/high commitment -> S4 delegating.
- For path-goal: unclear route -> directive; dull, frustrating, or isolating work -> supportive; followers need control or input -> participative; challenging work plus high expectations -> achievement-oriented. Do not duplicate structure already supplied by rules and clear tasks.
- For ethics, ask in order: Are people respected? served? treated justly? told the truth? included in a real community? Then check consequences, duties, and character.
- For a team, act only after locating the need: internal task, internal relational, external task/support, or external relationship. Performance without development and cohesion without results are both incomplete.

## Useful Thresholds And Tells

- Skills Inventory: 23-30 high, 14-22 moderate, 6-13 low. Use scores to choose practice, not to label potential.
- LMX 7: 30-35 very high, 25-29 high, 20-24 moderate, 15-19 low, 7-14 very low. Compare leader and follower ratings.
- Leadership Grid: 1-1 is impoverished, 5-5 middle-of-the-road, and 9-9 team management. Treat 9-9 as a development hypothesis, not a guaranteed winner.
- PLIS: reverse-score items 9 and 30; lower total means higher perceived integrity. It measures perception, not moral truth.
- Smell: title without influence, or power used for private ends, is not automatically leadership.
- Smell: delegating to an eager novice, adding direction to an already structured task, or using a group average to diagnose individuals.
- Smell: charismatic vision plus suppressed dissent = pseudotransformational risk; care without autonomy = paternalism; extra-role effort without opportunity or recognition = LMX clique.
- Smell: a technical training fix for an adaptive conflict, a psychodynamic label from one anecdote, a country score applied to an individual, or vague "fit" in promotion.

## Evidence Warnings

Use instruments for feedback and comparison, not as automatic prescriptions. Traits are situation-dependent; behavioral research has no single best style; SLII and path-goal prescriptions have partial or inconsistent support. Transformational, authentic, servant, and adaptive models are broad and have unresolved causal or measurement limits. Psychodynamic explanations are hypotheses, GLOBE measures perceived endorsement, and gender findings require attention to role, culture, and intersectionality.
