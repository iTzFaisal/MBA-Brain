# Glossary

**Abusive supervision** - Sustained verbal or nonverbal hostility toward subordinates (Ch 14).
**Balanced Scorecard** - Control view combining financial, customer, internal-process, and innovation/learning measures (Ch 16).
**Benchmarking** - Comparing performance with high-performing organizations or standards (Ch 6).
**Big Five** - Extroversion, agreeableness, conscientiousness, emotional stability, and openness (Ch 11).
**Break-even point** - Revenue level where total revenue equals total cost (Appendix).
**Career readiness** - Possession of competencies employers seek, developed through practice and feedback (Ch 1).
**Cascading goals** - Translating strategic goals into unit and individual goals (Ch 5).
**Communication noise** - Physical, psychological, semantic, or physiological disturbance in communication (Ch 15).
**Contingency viewpoint** - The best management approach depends on the situation (Ch 2).
**Corporate governance** - System protecting owners' and stakeholders' interests through oversight (Ch 3).
**Creating shared value (CSV)** - Improving competitiveness while advancing community economic and social conditions (LM 1).
**Decision tree** - Graph of decisions and possible consequences used to assess choices (Ch 7).
**Diversity Wheel** - Personality, internal, external, and organizational dimensions of difference (Ch 11).
**Emotional intelligence** - Using information about one's own and others' emotions to guide action (Ch 11).
**Expectancy** - Belief that effort will produce performance (Ch 12).
**Five forces** - New entrants, supplier power, buyer power, substitutes, and rivalry (Ch 6).
**Force-field analysis** - Identifying forces supporting and resisting a proposed change (Ch 10).
**Gantt chart** - Bar chart showing tasks against scheduled completion dates (Appendix).
**Globalization** - Increasing interdependence among national economies (Ch 4).
**Groupthink** - Pressure for agreement that prevents accurate assessment (Ch 7).
**Human capital** - Productive potential of employee knowledge, experience, and competencies (Ch 9).
**Innovation** - Creation of something new and useful that is commercialized (Ch 10).
**Job Characteristics Model** - Skill variety, task identity, task significance, autonomy, and feedback as motivational job attributes (Ch 12).
**Kohlberg's moral development** - Preconventional, conventional, and postconventional moral reasoning (Ch 3).
**Lewin's Change Model** - Unfreezing, changing, and refreezing planned change (Ch 10).
**LMX** - Leader-member exchange; leaders form different-quality relationships with followers (Ch 14).
**Management by objectives** - Joint objective setting, action planning, review, appraisal, and rewards (Ch 5).
**Media richness** - A medium's capacity to convey nuance, ambiguity, and immediate feedback (Ch 15).
**Mintzberg's roles** - Interpersonal, informational, and decisional roles managers perform (Ch 1).
**Motivation** - Psychological processes that arouse and direct goal-directed behavior (Ch 12).
**Organizational culture** - Shared, taken-for-granted assumptions shaping perception and behavior (Ch 8).
**Organizational justice** - Perceived fairness of distributions, procedures, and interpersonal treatment (Ch 12).
**Organizational structure** - Formal task and reporting relationships coordinating work (Ch 8).
**P-O fit** - Compatibility between a person's values and an organization's context (Ch 8).
**PDCA** - Plan-Do-Check-Act cycle for data-based continuous improvement (Ch 16).
**POLC** - Planning, organizing, leading, and controlling (Ch 1).
**Power** - Ability to marshal human, informational, and other resources (Ch 14).
**Prosocial behavior** - Voluntary behavior intended to benefit another person (Ch 11).
**Rational decision model** - Identify, generate, evaluate/select, implement/evaluate (Ch 7).
**Shared value** - Stakeholder benefits created alongside economic value (LM 1).
**SMART goal** - Specific, measurable, attainable, results-oriented, and time-bound (Ch 5).
**Social capital** - Productive potential of trusting and cooperative relationships (Ch 9).
**Stakeholder** - Person or group that affects or is affected by the organization (Ch 3).
**Strategic management** - Organization-wide formulation and implementation of strategy (Ch 6).
**Systems viewpoint** - Inputs transformed into outputs through interrelated processes and feedback (Ch 2).
**TQM** - Organization-wide continuous quality improvement, training, and customer focus (Ch 16).
**Triple bottom line** - People, planet, and profit as dimensions of success (Ch 3).
**Tuckman's model** - Forming, storming, norming, performing, and adjourning (Ch 13).
**Values** - Deeply held beliefs that guide thinking and behavior (Ch 3, Ch 11).
**VRIO** - Test whether a resource is valuable, rare, hard to imitate, and supported by organization (Ch 6).
